Guest software

Linux, BusyBox, and the RISC-V boot loader are separate guest components.
Their binaries are downloaded by the user's browser only after Boot and
are not redistributed by this project. Exact immutable URLs, observed
versions, byte lengths, SHA-256 checks, and available source references
are recorded in provenance.json.

Additional guest profiles

Alpine Linux 3.24.3 and Debian GNU/Linux 13.7 slim are fetched from
their official, immutable GitHub artifact commits on Boot. They are
not bundled with the application. See guest-profiles.json for exact
archive hashes, source/build metadata, and assembly details.

Alpine packages retain the SPDX identifiers and source recipe commits
in /lib/apk/db/installed. Debian package copyright/license files remain
under /usr/share/doc/*/copyright. The images are collections of separately
licensed software; the application and TinyEMU licenses do not replace
those licenses.

The Debian bootstrap copies two files from the verified Alpine archive
into the guest's memory filesystem:
- BusyBox 1.37.0-r31: GPL-2.0-only, aports source recipe commit
  c3ef5d10e6ef6528852c51f0564963e2f8c1be19.
- musl 1.2.6-r3 loader: MIT, aports source recipe commit
  8f79d39391b46d6dbf1800fb68b8212a21d5ee2e.
Source recipes, archive references, and patches:
https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports

Application-owned init scripts, the bounded tar/9p assembler, and runtime
adapters are Apache-2.0 integration code. They do not overwrite Debian's
glibc, Bash, apt, or package database. Guest components remain subject to
their own licenses if an operator chooses to redistribute a derived image.
